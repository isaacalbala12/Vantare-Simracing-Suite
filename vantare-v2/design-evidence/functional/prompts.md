Same sample race data across all three makes the style comparison clear. They should feel like three considered directions from an excellent automotive UI art director, not three color themes of the same table.

The three supplied images are reference context ONLY: first two show excellent compact on-track information density; third shows an existing Vantare Redline visual identity. Do NOT copy any layout, big logo header, manufacturer-logo tiles, red band, or existing distinctive composition. Carry the spirit of a serious racing instrument and recognisable Vantare quality into a genuinely new visual identity.

Session strip integrated with a discreet widely-spaced VANTARE wordmark. Visual distinction through typographic hierarchy and exquisite alignment, not decoration. One restrained machined corner detail. Driver row highlighted with a quiet light-slate plane, not a pill or thick border.

02 PACE: kinetic modern race broadcast. Dark ink translucent surfaces, slightly forward-leaning position numerals contrasting upright beautifully weighted driver names. A very restrained vermilion/salmon accent, unexpected compact asymmetrical header, cropped graphic sliver at the edge, meaningful negative space. A narrow side rhythm links the seven positions visually. Feels fast and confident, not cyberpunk. Do NOT recreate Redline red logo blocks or red horizontal class bar.

03 ATELIER: understated premium automotive editorial design. Charcoal with subtle warm undertone, ivory typography, a tiny pale champagne signature detail. Exceptional microtypography and vertical rhythm. Position column confidently spacious, driver column condensed but relaxed, timing slim tabular figures. Elegant offset micro brand label, beautiful optical balance, hairline separators only where useful. No brass gradients, luxury cliches or large ornamental frame.
